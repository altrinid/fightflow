# FAQ's

- Do I need any previous kickboxing experience?

Not at all. Our classes are suitable for complete beginners as well as experienced fighters. Our coaches will guide you through the techniques step by step and help you progress at your own pace.

- What should I bring to my first training?

Just comfortable sportswear, a water bottle, and a positive attitude. If you have your own gloves and hand wraps, feel free to bring them. If not, we can provide equipment for your first session.

- Do I need to be fit to start kickboxing?

No. You don't need to be in great shape before you start. Kickboxing is a great way to improve your fitness, strength, coordination, and endurance — and you'll get fitter as you train.

- Can I try a class before signing up?

Absolutely! We offer a FREE trial session so you can experience the training, meet the coaches, and see if our club is the right fit for you.

- Do I have to spar?

No. Sparring is not mandatory. You can train for fitness, technique, or personal development without sparring. If you want to spar, our coaches will introduce it gradually and safely.
