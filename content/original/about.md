# FightFlow - About

# About Our gym

FightFlow is a kickboxing club in Vienna, founded with a passion for martial arts, discipline and constant improvement. We are not just a gym - it’s a place where training brings people together, friendships are built, and everyone motivates and supports each other to become better.

Our Obmann & Head Coach is a professional coach in Kickboxing and Kyokushin Karate, a World Champion, 3× European Champion and multiple-time Russian Champion.

At FightFlow, we welcome everyone - from complete beginners to experienced fighters - and focus on technique, fitness, discipline and real progress.

Train hard. Stay disciplined. Keep moving.
